How the numbers are kept accurate

Tax rates, thresholds and allowances are stored per tax year with a link to the official GOV.UK page each figure comes from. An automated check compares them against GOV.UK and flags any change, so the calculators can be updated as soon as rates move. These tools are for guidance only and are not financial advice.
