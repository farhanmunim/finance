How the numbers
are kept accurate

Official figures in, every step out, checked every month.

  1. 01

    Official rates, with sources

    Tax rates, thresholds and allowances are stored per tax year, each linked to the GOV.UK page it comes from.
  2. 02

    Checked against GOV.UK monthly

    An automated check compares the stored figures with GOV.UK on the 1st of each month and flags any change for an update.
  3. 03

    Every step shown

    Results show how they were worked out, and the code is open source (opens in a new tab), so you can check the method yourself.

Questions people
ask

Short answers about how these tools work.

Is anything I type stored or sent?
No. The maths runs in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server, saved in a cookie or kept in local storage. Your inputs are written into the page address so you can bookmark a result; anyone you share that link with will see those figures.
Where do the tax rates come from?
From GOV.UK. Every figure is stored per tax year with a link to its official source, and a monthly automated check flags anything that has changed.
Is this financial advice?
No. The calculators are for guidance only. Check your own position with HMRC, your lender or a qualified adviser before making a decision.
Does it cost anything?
No. The tools are free, need no account and show no adverts. Page views are counted anonymously without cookies.
