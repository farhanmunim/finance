About this car finance calculator

Dealers and lenders show a finance quote as a list of numbers: the cash price, deposit, amount of credit, monthly payments, an optional final payment, the APR and the total amount payable. This calculator takes the handful of figures you can see and lays out what they mean, so you can see the cost of borrowing at a glance and compare quotes side by side.

What it shows

  • How much you borrow, how much you pay back and how much of that is interest
  • The interest as an average a month and a year, and how much falls in each year of the agreement
  • The effective APR worked out from the payments, so you can check the figure on the quote
  • The monthly rate and the flat rate, and why they differ from the APR
  • How much you still owe over time, including the large balance a PCP leaves until the final payment
  • What a bigger deposit or a lower APR would do to the monthly payment and the interest

How it is calculated

The calculator finds the monthly interest rate at which your payments exactly repay the amount borrowed. Monthly payments are made at the end of each month and the final payment in the last month of the term, which is how most UK quotes are laid out: a 48-month PCP is normally 47 monthly payments followed by the final payment in month 48. The APR is that monthly rate compounded over a year, as the consumer credit rules require, and includes any fees you enter. It does not cover excess mileage or damage charges at the end of a PCP, insurance or servicing. It is for guidance only and is not financial advice. Buying a home as well?
